# Module 0: Orientation and model building

> **Builds on:** nothing · **Next:** `01_charge_voltage_current.md` · **Widgets:** none needed

## Opening (new learners only)

Do not summarize the course files. Start the tutoring conversation.

Your first response should briefly set the contract: this will be a question-led course, it is fine to be wrong, the learner can pause for side questions, and progress is saved between sessions so they can stop at any time. Then ask one calibration question:

> In your own words, what do voltage and current mean, and how confident are you in that explanation?

Use the answer to choose the starting point. Do not skip the foundations just because the learner knows equations. Test whether the equations are tied to a physical picture. Record the answer in `memory/learner_profile.md`.

## Destination

The learner understands that the course will move between three levels:

- a physical level: charges, ions, fields, materials, and geometry;
- a circuit level: voltage sources, resistors, capacitors, and impedances;
- a biological or experimental level: membranes, channels, neurons, electrodes, amplifiers, and arrays.

They understand that a circuit diagram is a useful compressed model, not a claim that a cell contains tiny electronic components.

## Required moves

- Ask the learner to name one question they hope to answer by the end.
- Ask for their current picture of voltage and current.
- Establish that they may interrupt, ask "why," or follow a side branch.
- Explain that being able to predict and explain matters more than completing modules.

## Visual moments

- Optional: a small diagram (Mermaid or SVG) showing the same system at three levels, such as K⁺ ions crossing a membrane ↔ current through a conductance ↔ a membrane-potential change seen by an amplifier. Ask the learner to add a fourth example.

## Readiness check

Ask the learner to give an example of the same system described at two levels, such as "ions crossing a membrane" and "current through a conductance."

## Memory

- **Uses from memory:** none (first module). For a returning learner who is redoing Module 0, read `learner_profile.md`.
- **Record for later:**
  - `learner_profile.md`: background, their hoped-for end question, and their calibration answer.
  - `insights.md`: their starting picture of voltage and current, verbatim if possible. Module 1 checks it against later understanding, and the final synthesis shows how far they came.
