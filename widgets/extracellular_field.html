What an extracellular electrode sees

Each neuron is modeled as a current sink at the soma and an equal, opposite source at the end of its dendrite, in a uniform conductive medium. The electrode records potential relative to a reference. Modules 7 and 8.

Say what you expect and why.

Point-source approximation in an infinite, uniform, purely resistive medium, with no electrode interface, no filtering, and no noise. Real cells have distributed currents and complex geometry, and tissue is not uniform. Values are illustrative.
