  2. Freeze a suprathreshold spike, then double the amplitude. Is the spike twice as tall?

Say what you expect and why.

Classic Hodgkin-Huxley squid-axon parameters (6.3 °C), single compartment, so there is no propagation. Stimulus in µA/cm². Real mammalian neurons have more channel types and different kinetics. The Na⁺/K⁺ pump is not modeled, and it isn't needed to produce or end a spike.
