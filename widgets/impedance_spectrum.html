A lumped equivalent circuit summarizes behavior over a frequency range. It is not the literal microscopic structure, and different circuits (or parameter sets) can fit the same data. Real measurements also depend on amplitude, medium, temperature, counter/reference electrodes, cabling, and instrument range. Presets are illustrative orders of magnitude, not specifications or acceptance criteria.
